==================================================
11 — STACKING CHOREOGRAPHY
==========================

Pin the stack section.

Map approximately one viewport-height of scroll per transition, adjusted after testing.

Do not blindly use:

window.innerHeight * (totalCards - 1)

if the actual pacing feels too long.

The animation should behave approximately:

CARD 1 visible.

As CARD 2 arrives:
CARD 1 scale → ~0.92
rotation → subtle negative/positive angle
CARD 2 y: 100% → 0%

As CARD 3 arrives:
CARD 2 recedes slightly
CARD 1 remains deeper behind
CARD 3 becomes dominant.

Continue.

IMPORTANT:

Do not transform ALL old cards identically.

Create accumulated depth.

Example conceptual states:

immediately previous:
scale .92

two behind:
scale .88

older:
scale .85

But keep the visible stack restrained.

Do not create a huge fan of cards.

==================================================
12 — ROTATION LANGUAGE
======================

Do not rotate every previous card +5deg.

Use a controlled pattern such as:

Card 1: -2deg
Card 2: +2.5deg
Card 3: -1.5deg
Card 4: +2deg

Final card:
0deg

This creates controlled imperfection.

As FINAL FRAME arrives, the composition becoming perfectly aligned at 0deg should communicate resolution.

That is meaningful animation.

==================================================
13 — INTERNAL CARD MOTION
=========================

Each card can have ONE lightweight internal scroll-linked response.

Direction:
elements resolve toward focus.

Interface:
grid/layout lines settle.

Motion:
one trajectory progresses.

Engineering:
responsive frames align.

Final Frame:
image settles from ~1.025 scale to 1.

Do NOT create independent giant timelines for every child.

Internal animation should be subordinate to the stacking movement.

==================================================
14 — CARD TEXT BEHAVIOR
=======================

Text must remain readable throughout.

Suggested card composition:

upper-left:
micro label

lower-left:
large title

lower/right or upper-right:
supporting sentence

Visual occupies remaining space.

Do not put all information in the center.

Do not make these look like presentation slides.

Use the existing BoldFrame typography and metadata system.

Some titles can intentionally approach/crop against card edges.

==================================================
15 — ONE IMPORTANT DETAIL: PHYSICAL DEPTH
=========================================

Use subtle depth cues WITHOUT generic shadows.

Possible cues:

* tiny border contrast differences
* previous card visible around edges
* slight scale difference
* rotation
* environmental occlusion
* subtle background darkness behind stack

Avoid massive box-shadow blur.

The viewer should understand there are physical layers.

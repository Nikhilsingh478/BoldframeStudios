==================================================
06 — GSAP / SCROLLTRIGGER
==================================================

Use GSAP ScrollTrigger.

Pin the manifesto viewport while its track moves horizontally.

Calculate horizontal travel from REAL dimensions.

Conceptually:

horizontalDistance =
track.scrollWidth - viewportWidth

Do NOT hardcode arbitrary translateX values such as -500vw.

Use function-based GSAP values where appropriate so ScrollTrigger can recalculate after resize.

The movement must begin only when the section reaches the correct position.

Suggested behavior:

start: "top top"
pin: true
scrub: approximately 0.7-1.0
invalidateOnRefresh: true

Determine end distance from actual horizontal travel.

Do not make the scroll excessively long.

The user should feel momentum, not imprisonment.
